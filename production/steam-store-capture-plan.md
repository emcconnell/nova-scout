# Nova Scout — Steam Store Capture Plan

**Purpose:** Prepare screenshots, trailer footage, and store art direction after the game reaches a polished release-candidate state.

Do not capture final store assets until the first-five-minutes flow, star scanning, settings, and Mothership ending are implemented and stable.

---

## Store Positioning

**Short pitch:**

A retro sci-fi arcade survey shooter where every star scan is a dangerous discovery and the final beacon awakens a Mothership you must defeat to bring humanity home.

**Player-facing pillars:**

1. Dangerous star scanning.
2. Tight arcade space combat.
3. Five-sector story campaign.
4. Build-shaping upgrades and replay scoring.
5. 1950s-70s retrofuturist sci-fi style.

---

## Screenshot Requirements

Capture 8-12 screenshots from real gameplay, no debug overlays.

| Shot | Purpose | Required Content |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Identity | Title/menu or clean hero gameplay with readable HUD |
| 02 | First action | Player dodging asteroids, firing laser, collecting pickup |
| 03 | Star scan | Scan UI with orbit, pressure, and readable star label |
| 04 | Discovery | Human viable reveal or spectrum/planet presentation |
| 05 | Upgrade choice | Upgrade screen with current -> new stat comparison |
| 06 | Sector variety | Nebula/alien/frontier sector identity visible |
| 07 | Combat density | Mixed hazards/enemies, readable but exciting |
| 08 | Boss reveal | Mothership entrance or first phase |
| 09 | Boss climax | Mothership phase 3, core exposed, high drama |
| 10 | Score/replay | End-of-sector or victory scoring breakdown |

---

## Trailer Beat Sheet: 60-90 seconds

1. 0:00-0:05 — Cold open: Earth/humanity premise in one line.
2. 0:05-0:12 — Launch and arcade movement/combat.
3. 0:12-0:22 — Star scanning begins; signal interference rises.
4. 0:22-0:30 — Scan result reveal: barren/reward/alien warning.
5. 0:30-0:42 — Escalating sectors, hazards, mines, alien waves.
6. 0:42-0:52 — Upgrade choices and build identity.
7. 0:52-1:05 — Sector 5 pressure and final beacon.
8. 1:05-1:18 — Mothership reveal and phase montage.
9. 1:18-1:25 — Victory/score/title card.
10. 1:25-1:30 — Steam call-to-action.

---

## Capsule Art Brief

**Composition:**

- Survey Probe Seven foreground, angled upward/right.
- Huge Mothership silhouette in background.
- Star cluster/spectrum rings behind the probe.
- Retro sci-fi color palette: phosphor green, amber, magenta nebula, cream highlights.
- Readable title at small sizes.

**Avoid:**

- Generic realistic space sim look.
- Overly dark image where the ship disappears.
- UI screenshots as capsule art.
- Promising mechanics not visible in the game.

---

## Store Copy Checklist

- [ ] Mentions dangerous star scanning.
- [ ] Mentions five-sector campaign.
- [ ] Mentions arcade combat.
- [ ] Mentions upgrades/scoring if implemented.
- [ ] Mentions Mothership climax without spoiling all ending details.
- [ ] Does not claim one-hour runtime if target remains 35-45 minutes.
- [ ] Does not claim roguelite/procedural depth unless actually implemented.
- [ ] Does not claim accessibility/control features before they exist.

---

## Capture Preconditions

- [ ] Sector 5 Mothership ending flow is fixed.
- [ ] Star scanning pressure/reveal is polished enough for store footage.
- [ ] Upgrade UI looks final or close to final.
- [ ] Settings can disable/reduce excessive screen shake/flash for capture.
- [ ] HUD is readable in screenshots.
- [ ] Performance is stable enough for clean video capture.

---

## Post-Capture Review

For each candidate screenshot/trailer clip, ask:

- Does this communicate the game's unique hook?
- Is the action readable at Steam thumbnail size?
- Does it look finished rather than prototype/debug?
- Is there variety across the set?
- Is every visible feature actually in the release build?
